This project demonstrates a more sophisticated approach to modern headlight technology. Beyond mandatory low and high beams, it uses the flexibility of LEDs to create a safer driving environment under low-light conditions. The main objective is to illuminate as much of the road as possible while not dazzling other drivers — for both oncoming and preceding traffic.

This is achieved with two sub-systems in communication: a hardware module controlling the LED matrix, and a camera feed with a trained object detection model to detect vehicles, locate them, and determine where to lighten or darken.
